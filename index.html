GET TO KNOW US

A place where every child can flourish.

Magic Steps is a multidisciplinary child development centre and family wellness space. We bring together occupational therapy, speech therapy, ABA, behavioural therapy, special education and psychology.

We believe progress happens when children, families, teachers and specialists work as one team. Our approach is centred on understanding each child's needs and supporting their individual journey.
